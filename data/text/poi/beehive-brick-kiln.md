The only remaining “beehive” brick-making kiln — once operated by imprisoned laborers at the Lorton Workhouse. Between 1910 and 1967, this oven was one of nine that produced millions of red bricks used to construct public buildings in D.C. and northern Virginia. It stands 15 feet tall, 30 feet across, with an adjacent 40-foot chimney. Visitors are welcome to peer inside and wander the historic site.

Accotink Creek was once the site of several grist mills, powered by the creek's flow, that were important to the area's early development and economy.
 
Remnants of a 19th-century mill race can still be found here, with signage posted along the trail. Daniel McCarty Chichester — married to a cousin of George Washington's — built a dam on Accotink Creek around 1801 to power a grist mill and sawmill; the mill race is still visible.

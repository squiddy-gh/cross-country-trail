***Parking tip:  The trailhead lot on Hooes Road only fits a handful of cars. As an alternative, there's a large commuter lot on Gambrill Road, across from the mini-mart — it's an extra half mile walk to the trailhead, but you won't have to turn around. On weekdays, there's limited commuter buses servicing the Pentagon, Franconia-Springfield Metro, and Lorton VRE to//from here. ***

The mini-mart at the corner of Gambrill and Hooes Road is the only place to buy food and drink between Liberty Market in Lorton and the Ravensworth Shopping Center in Springfield — fill your backpack here if you haven't already. The grab-and-go case has sandwiches and an unusually large assortment of nonalcoholic beverages, including hot and cold coffee. Hot dogs and taquitos are also on the menu.

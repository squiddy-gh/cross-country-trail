The Accotink stream valley was once part of the largest land grant in northern Virginia — 21,966 acres given to John Matthews, an original Jamestown grantee, later purchased by William Fitzhugh. The Palladian-style mansion known as Ravensworth, built on the property by Fitzhugh's great-grandson, was said to be one of the most imposing residences in Fairfax County until it burned in 1926. Look for the historical markers just off the trail at the Ravensworth shopping plaza on Braddock Road.

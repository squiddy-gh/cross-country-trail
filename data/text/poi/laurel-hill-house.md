The abandoned home of Revolutionary War hero William Lindsay has stood for more than 200 years. Built on a ridge top between 1787–1790, the small frame house changed considerably over the 19th and 20th centuries, obscuring its original form. It served as the prison superintendents' residence from 1914 to 1972 and has sat empty for over half a century. Though listed on the National Register of Historic Places, it's now in poor condition, surrounded by a chain-link security fence.
